What does Amazon PPC automation include?

The daily routine of running Amazon ads, handled for you and explained in plain English.

01

Keyword research

We find the search terms shoppers use for your product, group them by intent and rank them, so campaigns start with a clear plan.

02

Campaigns that are easy to read

Campaigns and ad groups are organized by product and match type, so you can understand your account at a glance.

03

Bid checks every morning

Bids and search terms are reviewed each day. Terms that waste spend are paused and terms that convert get a closer look.

04

Plain-English notes

How it works

How does PPC automation work, from first search term to weekly report?

Four steps. You stay in charge of the budget the whole way.

  1. 01

    Research the keywords

    We collect search terms for your product, score how well they fit and group them by what the shopper wants.

  2. 02

    Set up the campaigns

    Campaigns and ad groups are built around your products, with clear names and budgets you approve first.

  3. 03

    Check bids every morning

    Each day we review bids and search terms, pause what wastes money and raise what works, inside your limits.

  4. 04

    Get a note in plain English

    You receive a short note on what changed and why, a weekly summary and the next thing we suggest.

FAQ

Questions about PPC automation

Not here? Book a free audit and ask us directly.

What does Amazon PPC automation include?

Keyword research, campaign setup, daily bid and search-term checks, and a plain-English note after each change. A person on our team reviews the work, and you get a short weekly summary.

Will you spend my budget without asking?

No. You set the budget and the limits. Routine checks stay inside them, and bigger changes come to you for approval first.

Which ad platforms do you cover?

We start with Amazon advertising. Other marketplace ad tools can be added when your business needs them.

Can you promise sales results?

No one can honestly promise that. We promise a clear routine, clear notes and no surprises. Results also depend on your product, price, reviews and listing quality, which is why we look at those too.
