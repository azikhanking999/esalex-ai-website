How it works

How do we turn product details into live listings?

Four steps, and a person is involved in every one of them.

  1. 01

    Send your product details

    Share a sheet, your current listings or your supplier data. We read it, spot the gaps and agree on your brand voice.

  2. 02

    AI drafts every listing

    Titles, bullets, descriptions and keywords are drafted for each product in your voice, in one batch.

  3. 03

    A person checks each one

    We check facts, claims, tone and marketplace rules. You review a first sample before we go further.

  4. 04

    Upload and keep improving

    We prepare the bulk upload file, publish when you say yes, then tune the copy as you learn what sells.

FAQ

Questions about listing automation

Not here? Book a free audit and ask us directly.

What is AI listing automation?

It is a setup where AI drafts the repetitive parts of your listings from your product data: titles, bullets, descriptions, keywords and the upload file. It writes in your brand voice, and a person on our team checks every draft before it goes live.

Which marketplaces can you prepare listings for?

Amazon, Shopify and Walmart are the most common. We also prepare files for eBay, Etsy, TikTok Shop and Overstock.

Will the listings sound like my brand?

Yes. We start from your current listings, brand notes and product sheets, write a short voice guide, and you approve a first batch before we go further.

Can you update a whole catalog, not just new products?

Yes. We work in batches. You review a sample, then we prepare the bulk upload file for the rest, so nothing changes in your store without your approval.

How do we get started?

Book a free audit. We look at your listings and catalog, point out where drafting by hand costs the most time and suggest where to begin.
