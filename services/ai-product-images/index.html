FAQ

Questions about product images and A+

Not here? Book a free audit and ask us directly.

What are A+ content and detail-page images?

Detail-page images are the gallery pictures on an Amazon listing: the main image, infographics, comparison charts and lifestyle shots. A+ content is the rich section further down the page, made of banners and modules that tell the product story.

How is AI-made imagery different from regular design?

AI speeds up scene building, backgrounds and variations. A designer then checks product shape, color, labels and text, and fixes anything that is off. Your product always stays your product.

How many images are in a set?

A common set is seven images for the listing gallery, plus A+ modules when you want them. We agree the exact set in the plan before we start.

Do you work from my own product photos?

Yes. Send your photos and any brand files. If you have no photos yet, we can talk about options on the audit call.
