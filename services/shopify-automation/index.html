How it works

How do we automate a store workflow without breaking it?

We start with one workflow, prove it works, then add the next.

  1. 01

    Map what happens today

    We follow one workflow from start to finish, such as an order coming in, and note every manual step on the way.

  2. 02

    Connect your tools

    Your store, marketplaces, sheets and inbox are linked up. You keep the tools you already use.

  3. 03

    Automate, with a person in the loop

    The routine steps run on their own. Anything unusual, like an odd refund, is sent to a person.

  4. 04

    Watch, fix and add the next one

    We watch the activity log, fix what needs fixing and queue the next workflow when you are ready.

FAQ

Questions about workflow automation

Not here? Book a free audit and ask us directly.

Which tasks can you automate in an ecommerce store?

The repetitive ones: moving order details between tools, updating stock, drafting replies to common customer questions, and pulling together weekly reports. If your team copies and pastes the same thing every day, it is a good candidate.

Do I have to change the tools my team already uses?

No. We connect the tools you already have, such as your store, marketplaces, spreadsheets and inbox, so your team keeps working the way it does today.

Do you build Shopify stores as well?

Yes. We have built Shopify stores from scratch, including one with more than 1,000 products and one with a built-in design tool for custom printing. We handle the catalog, collections and admin setup.

What happens when something unusual comes in?

It is flagged for a person. Refunds over your limit, odd orders and upset customers are never handled by guesswork.
